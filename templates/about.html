About SmartMerge

SmartMerge is a high-performance, client-first document utility designed for professionals, educators, and students who need fast, secure document processing without bloatware or cloud subscription fees.

Technology Stack

Python 3
Backend Engine
Flask
Micro-Framework
PyPDF2 & PIL
Document Processing
Glass CSS
Visual Theme

Privacy & Security Guarantee

SmartMerge processes all files locally on your machine server. Temporary files generated during merge/split operations are purged immediately upon delivery.
